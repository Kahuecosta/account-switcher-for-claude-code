# Switch accounts

- Click the account in the **status bar**.
- Use the **Claude Accounts** view in the Activity Bar.
- Press `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+A` (`Cmd+Alt+Shift+A` on macOS) to go back to the previous account.

Each window can use a different account. The Claude Code panel and its new chats follow right away, without reloading the window; conversations already open keep their account until you close them. With an older Claude Code, the window reloads instead, and the status bar shows a sync icon while a reload is pending.
